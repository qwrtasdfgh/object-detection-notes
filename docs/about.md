# About Dr. Jialin Hou

I am Jialin Hou, a researcher with a background in data science. My doctoral research in Computer Science at the University of Regina examined how set-theoretic approaches can support the analysis of uncertainty and decision-making. I successfully defended my PhD thesis, *Set-Theoretical Models of Three-Way Data Analytics*, in August 2026.

I am now developing my research in object detection and computer vision. I am interested in understanding detection models, evaluating their limitations, and designing reproducible experiments that are practical with limited computing resources.

I created Object Detection Notes to organize and share the concepts, tools, and papers I study. The site brings together introductory explanations, practical tutorials, and research notes, with references that help readers explore the original work.
